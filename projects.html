Simulation Model for SNSPD

Undergraduate Research Intern, Rochester, NY

Laboratory for Laser Energetics (LLE) May 2025 – August 2025

  • Developed LTspice simulation model for superconducting nanostripe single-photon detectors (SNSPDs) by applying a lumped-element approach to simulate the electrical response of multi-photon detection events.
  • Designed a Single Flux Quantum (SFQ) readout circuit for the detector, using primitive cells DC-SFQ, SFQ-DC converters and SFQ flip-flops, to resolve analog waveforms up to four photon numbers.

Headphone Amplifier Circuit

  • Researched OPA1656 and BUF634A chips and tested with proper component values using theoretical calculations (resistors and capacitors), designed successful two-stage amplifier.
  • Soldered components together & tested amplifier using signal oscilloscope.
  • Used HSPICE to simulate specific test decks including first & second stage gain with bandwidth, input and & output impedance, transient power & THD.

DC Motor Control

  • Used Embedded C, 16/32 Explorer Board w/PIC32MX795F512L chip, MATLAB.
  • Programmed DC Motor Control in C, Integrated hardware components: 99:1 Metal Gearmotor 25Dx54L and Dual MC33926 Motor Driver Carrier for Motor Control, Implemented UART communication between board and MATLAB Program.
  • Configured Output comparison for generating precise PWM signals to control motor timing.
  • Designed Analog-to-Digital Converter function to accurately read potentiometer values, setting digital value as duty cycle for speed control of the DC gear motor while recording PID for the first 10 seconds.
