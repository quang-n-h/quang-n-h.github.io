Projects

MIPS 32-bit 5-Stage Pipelined Processor

2025

Designed a 32-bit MIPS processor in Verilog with the classic five-stage pipeline: instruction fetch, decode, execute, memory, and write-back. Built the datapath and control unit to run a subset of the MIPS instruction set, and added forwarding and hazard-detection logic so the pipeline resolves data and control hazards without producing wrong results. Verified the design with testbenches in simulation.

GitHub Repository

Semiconductor Devices

Fall 2025

Wrote a survey paper on semiconductor single-photon detectors, focusing on single-photon avalanche diodes (SPADs). Explained how a reverse-biased diode driven past its breakdown voltage turns one absorbed photon into a measurable current pulse, and compared device structures along with their dark-count and afterpulsing behavior and typical applications.

Final Term Paper

Headphone Amplifier Circuit

Spring 2025

Designed a multi-stage headphone amplifier and verified it in HSPICE, writing separate test decks to measure bandwidth, output power, and total harmonic distortion (THD). Built the amplifier on a physical board and confirmed it matched the simulated performance.

DC Motor Control

Fall 2024

HEX Bit Stream Encryption

Spring 2023

Designed a small finite-state machine that encrypts a hex bit stream. Worked through the state graph, next-state table, and Karnaugh maps to derive the flip-flop input equations, then built the circuit from three D flip-flops and logic gates, using a pushbutton (LEDR[0]) as the clock edge.
