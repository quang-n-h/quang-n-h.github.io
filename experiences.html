Research

Superconducting Photon-Number-Resolving Readout

Georgia Tech & University of Rochester, Summer 2025 - Present

Built an electrothermal LTspice model that reproduces the multi-photon response of a single superconducting nanowire single-photon detector (SNSPD), capturing how the intrinsic detector output changes with the number of photons absorbed at the same time. Designed and simulated rapid single-flux-quantum (RSFQ) circuits in PSCAN2 to convert that analog detector pulse into digital photon-number information.

Currently building a demultiplexer in SFQ logic - using DC-to-SFQ converters, AND cells, splitters, and coincidence correlators - that takes one SNSPD input pulse and routes it to separate output lines, one per number of simultaneous photons. This gives on-chip photon-number resolution, which can be used to characterize single-photon sources and to support protocols such as quantum teleportation and photonic quantum computing.
